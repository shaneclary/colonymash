# Future feature archive: Cabana Lab / community flavor creation
Status: **Not part of the launch experience. Proposed for 2027 or later, only if Erik approves.**

## Why archived
The practical launch priorities are the verified current rotation, quick requests for packaged cans and kegs, visit information, and genuine community engagement. Community-driven recipe/flavor contests are not required for the first launch and could complicate the owner's decision to adopt the website.

## Preserve the concept
- Tropical field-research lab with botanical specimen art, archival labels, a mid-century tropical aesthetic and subtle tiki-inspired geometry (without caricatures of Pacific Island cultures).
- A future workflow for proposing fruit / accent combinations, naming ideas and collaborative creative projects.
- Human brewer oversight: all proposed ingredients and any actual formulations remain subject to Erik's technical and creative approval.
- Privacy, moderation, intellectual-property permissions, participant terms, age requirements and abuse prevention before receiving public submissions.
- No invented production dates, health claims, launched products or votes.

The full previous interactive prototype and its styling are preserved in the Git history before this change, including commit f57c3ca7281dc77d604d88ede589bfdc1f5f02cc, in \`src/components/V3Experience.tsx\` and \`src/app/v3.css\`. The new launch preview does not render that prototype.

## Phased return
2026 launch: current verified flavors, packaged requests, visits, culture, content and existing social channels.
2027 potential pilot: private tasting feedback / staff-approved flavor preference surveys.
Later: Cabana Lab experiments and curated co-creation only with actual demand and owner consent.
